## You may, without asking

- Use "FlowKit" **nominatively** to state a fact: "built with FlowKit",
  "compatible with FlowKit", "a FlowKit adapter", "migrating from FlowKit".
- Keep the name in **unmodified** copies of the source you redistribute.
- Name FlowKit in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and reviews.
- Use the name for a **plugin/adapter/theme/integration** as a descriptive

  own name first.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "FlowKit" or "Flowlab" (or a confusingly similar name) as **the name of
  your product, company, service or domain**, or as an npm scope/package name
  that implies it is official.
- Use the **logos** in a modified form, or in a way that implies endorsement,
  affiliation or official status.
- Name a **fork** "FlowKit" or "FlowKit \<something\>". Pick a distinct name;
  you may say "a fork of FlowKit".
- Use the marks on **merchandise**, or register them (or similar marks) as a
  trademark, anywhere.
- Suggest the maintainer or Flowlab **sponsors or endorses** your project.

## Forks

Forks are allowed by the license. Rename the project, the package scope and the
CLI. A one-line "based on FlowKit (\<link\>)" is fine and encouraged;
"FlowKit-branded" is not.
