# FlowKit commercial license

FlowKit 1.x is source-available under the **PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0**
([`LICENSE.md`](./LICENSE.md)). Most use is free — see
[`LICENSING.md`](./LICENSING.md). A **separate commercial license** from the Licensor
lifts the license's **Noncompete** term for a named product. The Shield license is
permanent and never converts to an open-source license, so a commercial license is the
only way to build a competing offering on FlowKit.

## When you need one

You need a commercial license if you want to:

- **Compete with Flowlab or FlowKit** — offer a hosted, managed or cloud service
  that lets third parties build, publish, host or collect responses for guided
  flows, multi-step forms or surveys.
- **White-label** FlowKit — ship a flow/form builder under your own brand for
  your customers or resellers.
- **OEM** FlowKit — embed FlowKit's builder/runtime into a product you license
  to other companies as their form/flow capability.
- **Embed FlowKit in a competing product** — bundle it inside an offering whose
  value proposition overlaps with Flowlab's.
- Distribute FlowKit under **different license terms** than PolyForm Shield 1.0.0.
- Get a **written warranty, indemnity, support SLA or legal sign-off** that the
  Shield license's "as is" terms do not provide.

You do **not** need one for: using FlowKit as a dependency in your own
(non-competing) product, internal tools, consulting, or
plugins/adapters/themes/custom steps.

## What a commercial license can grant

Negotiated per agreement. Typical terms:

- A perpetual, non-exclusive right to use FlowKit for a **named competing or
  white-label/OEM product**.
- Redistribution rights under terms you need.
- Optional: priority support, a support SLA, a defined maintenance window,
  roadmap input, indemnification.
- Pricing: flat annual fee, per-seat, per-deployment or revenue-share —
  depends on scale and use.

including:

1. Your company and the product involved.

3. Hosted / self-hosted / white-label / OEM / embedded.

You will get a response with whether a commercial license is needed and, if so,
proposed terms.

## Custom agreements

Non-standard needs — dual licensing, source escrow, a bespoke field-of-use
carve-out, government/regulated deployment terms — are handled case by case
through the same contact.
